Bamboo Forest 1 & 2

SleepsBedsCoolingBathroom
2 peopleDouble bedAir conditioningPrivate ensuite bathroom

The Rabbit Tree's deluxe rooms boast a sophisticated design and private ensuite bathroom. The Bamboo Forest 1 is located on the first floor on top of the garden and sport area. The Bamboo Forest 2 is located in a separate building to the main hostel, less than 10 seconds walk away. Of course you are still welcome for breakfast and use of all the facilities in the main building. For those looking for a serene, tranquil stay on Gili Meno, this is the perfect choice.
