What To Do In Gili Meno?

Who said there was nothing to do on this island?

There are activities every day at the Rabbit Tree. It would be hard to include everything we do together, but let's give a few examples... In Gili Meno there is a lot to do, and it only takes one hour — or one day if you slow down and take your time to enjoy the wonderful places the island offers.

At the hostel, every day

Movie night

The main sitting area turns into a cinema.

Yoga & workout

Start the day moving with the group.

Sunset on the beach

Five minutes walk from the dorm.

Traditional dinner

Eat with the staff and the locals.

Games night

Board games, cards and silly challenges.

Walk around the island

One hour by bike, a day if you stop often.

On the island

Discover the lake and the mangroves

Relax on the most lovely beach of the three Gilis, admire the sunrise or the sunset, and follow the lake path through the mangroves.

Visit the turtle sanctuary

A place near the beach with four small pools. The aim is to collect the eggs from the shore before predators eat them, grow the little turtles until they are about a year old, and release them in their natural environment. Thanks to this work there are still a lot of turtles around Gili Meno. You can support it with a small donation.

Join the Trash Heroes

Meet locals and travellers every Sunday around 3.30pm at the Brotherhood and walk together to collect the trash. It is an unforgettable moment to share with locals. They also take care of the trash bins around the island, so be sure to throw your waste in one of those.
