Pockets of dreams made into a reality

In the heart of Gili Meno's paradise you'll find The Rabbit Tree, a unique hostel oozing creativity and pockets of dreams made into a reality. Every square inch of the property has been intricately designed to provide guests with a playful and thought provoking experience.

Since opening in January 2017 the hostel has continued to grow and new details are added all the time. The Rabbit Tree provides comfortable and weather-proof rooms, fresh water showers, wifi, a long list of entertainment facilities, drinking water, tea, coffee and breakfast. You can also explore the secret passageways and hangout spots hidden around the facility.
