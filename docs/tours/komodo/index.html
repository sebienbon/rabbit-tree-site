Komodo Trip

4 days, 3 nights on a wooden boat through north Sumbawa and the Komodo Islands

The tour is a full live-on-board sailing experience with many activities such as snorkelling, hiking, hunting for the Komodo dragons and searching for the whale sharks. There is loads of time for sunbathing, sharing stories, watching endless panorama views, seeing multiple sunrises and sunsets, lots of sailing and swimming. Every boat includes its own tour guide who will take you to the amazing spots.

Departures on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Check-in between 8.30 and 10.00 at Bangsal Harbour.

Program

Day 1 — Bangsal, Kayangan Harbour, Kenawa Island

After a briefing we have a 2 hour bus drive (depending on traffic) to east Lombok where the boat departs. Activities: swimming, sailing, hiking.

Day 2 — Whale shark point (Saleh Bay, Sumbawa)

On the hunt for the whale sharks during snorkelling. Activities: swimming, sailing, sunbathing.

Day 3 — Komodo Park, Pink Beach, Long Beach, Padar Island

Visiting the legendary Komodo dragons during a guided hike at Komodo Park. Watch out, before you know it you are standing eye to eye with a Komodo dragon. Hiking the hills on Padar Island for sunset and sunrise on the boat.

Day 4 — Manjarite, Kelor Island, Labuan Bajo

Sailing towards the islands, island hopping for snorkelling, sunbathing, beach games and relaxing. Arriving in Labuan Bajo between 14.00 and 15.00, depending on the weather and the current.

The return from Labuan Bajo (Flores) is not included. Two companies offer the same program and service in a similar boat; the real difference is their communication skills, clarity of organisation, and the price.

Included

  • Three Indonesian meals a day (vegetarian and vegan options available) and snacks
  • Unlimited coffee, tea and mineral water; a fruit plate for breakfast and fresh fruit juice once a day
  • Entrance fee to Komodo National Park and an English speaking guide
  • Bus to the departure port, and boat tickets from Gili Trawangan and Gili Air
  • Mask and snorkel, plus traditional simple fishing gear
  • Beanbags, and charging points for electronic devices (solar energy)
  • Fresh water and a simple shower, lockers for your valuables, a cool box with ice for your extra drinks
  • Safety equipment: lifejackets, life raft and ring lifebuoy

Not included

  • Wifi connection
  • Fins
  • Pick up from Kuta Lombok (negotiate transport with the agent you book from)
  • The way back from Labuan Bajo
