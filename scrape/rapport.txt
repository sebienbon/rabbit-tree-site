Check now our offers and contact us, we'll make the bookings for you. ​ From Bali (All South Bali, Amed, Sanur, Nusa Penida/Lembongan etc.. ) : - Everyday : Daily minibus pick up you in the biggest city of Bali to join Padang Bai. - After : Daily ferry to join Gili Trawangan (or Gili Air). After, you have to take a local boat to join Gili Meno. ​ ​ We have the cheapest prices possible. Have a look now! ​ ​ From Gili Trawangan: - Daily public boat at 09:30 & 16:00 (40,000 idr) - Every hour by fast boat (85,000 idr) ​ From Lombok: - Public boat at 10:00, 14:00 and 17:00 (25,000 idr) - Every hour by fast boat (85,000 idr) How To Find The Rabbit Tree ? Once on Gili Meno: ​ Once arrived at the Gili Meno harbour, turn right and follow the coast road until you reach 'Karma Reef' restaurant. Go left and then right leading towards the back of the restaurant, briefly follow this until you reach a tree with many signs on it. You should see a sign pointing left for The Rabbit Tree. Follow the road on the left for about 5 minutes and you will find our hostel on the left side of the road just past Amigos Bungalows. ​ If you need any help, give us a call or ask any local! Visit the turtle sanctuary It’s a place near the beach with 4 small pools. The aim is to collect the eggs from the shore before predators eat them, grow the little turtles until they are about 1 year old, and release them in their natural environment. Thanks to this work, we still have a lot of turtles around Gili Meno. You can support this with a small donation! Join the Trash Heroes Meet locals and tourists every Sunday around 3.30pm at the Brotherhood and walk together to collect the trash. It will be an unforgettable moment to share with locals!
